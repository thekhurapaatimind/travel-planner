Jatayu Earth Centre

World's largest bird sculpture (Confirmed)

  • Cable car up and down (~8 min each way). Skip the adventure zones to save time.
  • Budget ~₹500 per person for entry + ropeway; confirm at the counter.
  • Ticket counter closes ~4:30 PM and crowds are heavy. Leave at 4:30 sharp for sunset.

Cafe & Beach Guide

Sunrise Walk on Black Beach

Quiet before the crowds. Back by 8:15 for breakfast.

Breakfast: Coffee Temple + Scooty Pickup

Cold brews on North Cliff, then grab the scooties

Scooties: ~₹400–800/day. Bring licence, ID and deposit; use authorised shops only.
Scooties can't go on the cliff walkway, so park at the designated spots.
10:00 AM

Janardhana Swamy Temple & Papanasam Beach

2,000-year-old temple, then a swim at the main beach

Swim near the lifeguards. The beach is crowded in the morning during temple rituals.
01:00 PM

Lunch: Darjeeling Cafe

North Cliff • sea views

Long lazy lunch out of the midday sun.
02:30 PM

Scooty Ride: Kappil or Anjengo

Pick one on the day

  • Kappil Beach & Lake (~7 km): beach and backwater in one spot, quiet and golden.
  • Anjengo Fort (~12 km): 17th-century British fort and fishing village.
  • Optional ~4:45 PM: Swayamvara Silks, Maithanam, for Kerala Kasavu sarees (check closing time).
05:45 PM

Sunset: Trip is Life Cafe, South Cliff

Quieter cliff, popular sunset spot

Sunset round two, from the other side of the cliff.
07:30 PM

Dinner: InDa Cafe

Lots of veg & vegan • backup: Mamma Chompas

Pack tonight. Checkout is 5:30 AM Monday.
