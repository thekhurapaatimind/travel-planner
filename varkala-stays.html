Varkala Stays

Most Recommended Hotels for 3 Rooms (Nov 28 - Nov 30)

Elixir Cliff Beach Resort

Luxury Pick
  • Unparalleled, secluded location directly on the cliff's edge offering panoramic views.
  • Sprawling property with a spectacular infinity pool overlooking the Arabian Sea.
  • Incredible aesthetic rooms perfect for our group to relax in extreme comfort.

Clafouti Beach Resort

Classic Mid-Range
  • Located right in the heart of the North Cliff action.
  • Direct ocean views with easy, private steps leading down to the beach.
  • Features one of the best in-house sea-view restaurants for lazy sunsets.

InDa Hotel

Eco-Boutique
  • Aesthetic rustic property surrounded by lush greenery, providing a perfect tropical vibe.
  • Home to the famous InDa Cafe right on the premises for amazing breakfasts.
  • A quiet sanctuary just a 5-minute walk inland from the bustling cliff.
