Varkala Cafe & Beach Plan

Sat evening + Sun • No cafe twice

Day 1 • Sat, Nov 28 • Evening
01

Bindu's Cafe • Sunset (06:00 PM)

The North Cliff spot where everyone gathers for sunset. Order the coconut-milk cappuccino.

02

Cliff Walk & Night Market (07:15 PM)

Silver jewellery, linen and souvenir stalls along the pedestrian cliff path.

03

Cafe del Mar • Dinner (08:00 PM)

Top-rated on the cliff, near the helipad. Seafood and veg; good for a big group.

Day 2 • Sun, Nov 29
04

Coffee Temple • Breakfast (08:30 AM)

Cold brews and breakfast bowls on North Cliff. Pick up the scooties right after.

05

Janardhana Swamy Temple & Papanasam Beach (10:00 AM)

2,000-year-old Vishnu temple, then down the steps for a swim near the lifeguards.

06

Darjeeling Cafe • Lunch (01:00 PM)

Sea views from North Cliff and a long, lazy lunch out of the midday sun.

07

Kappil Beach or Anjengo Fort (02:30 PM)

Scooty ride: Kappil (~7 km) for beach meets backwater, or Anjengo (~12 km) for the old British fort. Optional Kasavu saree stop at Swayamvara Silks.

08

Trip is Life Cafe • Sunset (05:45 PM)

South Cliff, quieter side. Sunset round two.

09

InDa Cafe • Dinner (07:30 PM)

Lots of veg and vegan options. Backup: Mamma Chompas for paneer and momos. Pack tonight; checkout is 5:30 AM Monday.
