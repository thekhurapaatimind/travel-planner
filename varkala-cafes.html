Day 2 Route

Sip. Surf. Savor. Stroll.

01

Coffee Temple (09:00 AM)

Morning fuel. Ocean views. Exceptional coffee and hearty breakfast bowls right on the edge of the North Cliff.

02

Trip is Life Cafe (10:30 AM)

Aesthetic architecture and unbeatable cliffside views. Perfect for mid-morning aesthetics and fresh coastal breezes.

03

Papanasam Beach (12:00 PM)

Walk down the cliff steps. Good for swimming, relaxing, and beginner surfing under the sun.

04

Darjeeling Cafe (02:00 PM)

Bohemian vibes and incredible seafood. The definitive Varkala lunch experience.

05

Black Sand Beach (04:30 PM)

A quick scooty ride north to Thiruvambadi Beach. Volcanic sands and a quieter shoreline.

06

Cliff Sunset & Dinner (06:30 PM)

Iconic sunset views followed by authentic wood-fired pizza at Cafe Italiano or Abba Restaurant, ending with souvenir shopping.
