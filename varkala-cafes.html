Day 2 Optimized Route

Eat Beach Eat Beach Sunset

01

Coffee Temple (09:00 AM)

Fuel up with a hearty breakfast right on the North Cliff overlooking the Arabian Sea. Exceptional coffee and nourishing breakfast bowls.

02

Papanasam Beach (10:30 AM)

Descend the cliff steps to the iconic main beach. Perfect for a refreshing morning swim, relaxing on the golden sand, and beginner surfing.

03

Trip is Life Cafe (01:00 PM)

Head to this vibrant, traveler-favorite cafe known for incredible bohemian ambiance, healthy fusion meals, and ice-cold smoothies.

04

Thiruvambadi Beach / Black Sand Beach (03:30 PM)

Hop on your rented scooties for a scenic coastal ride up north to the quieter Black Sand Beach for an intimate afternoon photoshoot and relaxing waves.

05

Darjeeling Cafe & Cliff Sunset (05:30 PM)

Return to the North Cliff to settle into Darjeeling Cafe or Cafe Italiano for the legendary Varkala sunset, followed by evening city shopping along the cliff pathway.
